## About GameKeys.Deals

[GameKeys.Deals](https://gamekeys.deals/) helps PC players compare game-key prices across multiple stores and find the best available offers.

Our goal is to make game-price comparison clearer by showing important differences between stores, regions, editions, activation methods, and product types.

## What we work on

- PC game-key price comparison
- Current and historical price tracking
- Store and marketplace integrations
- Game, edition, platform, and region matching
- Price validation and data-quality monitoring
- Buying-safety resources
- PC gaming market research

## Clearer price comparisons

A low listing price does not always represent the final or most suitable offer. GameKeys.Deals helps players distinguish between:

- Game keys, Steam Gifts, and account-based products
- Global and region-restricted offers
- Standard, Deluxe, Complete, and other editions
- Listed prices and final prices that may include service fees
- Direct key retailers and third-party marketplaces

## Research and data

We use our collected pricing data to study how PC game prices differ across stores and how major sales affect the market.

Our research may include:

- Steam prices compared with third-party key prices
- The effect of marketplace service fees
- Regional availability and activation restrictions
- Price differences between game editions
- Historical price movements and major sales
